          Forecast · Will each dossier make its target cycle?

          Probability of a complete upload by the department’s internal deadline. Select a row to see why.

          What drives the risk

          Model weights, scaled. Left lowers the chance of making the deadline. Right raises it.

          Model card. Logistic regression on five inputs. Fit on 3 years of synthetic dossier history (412 dossiers). Holdout AUC 0.84. Predictions fall within 6 points of observed rates in every band.

          Forecast · When will the missing evaluator letters arrive?

          Monte Carlo simulation, 4,000 runs per dossier, using each letter’s time since request. Bars show the 10th to 90th percentile date the last needed letter arrives. Dot is the median. Red tick is the date letters are needed.

          So what

          Model card. Reply times follow a log-normal curve, fit on synthetic history. Unmodified-rank letters: 62% reply, median 38 days. Modified-rank letters: 78% reply, median 28 days. A letter not yet received has a lower chance of ever arriving, and the simulation accounts for that.
